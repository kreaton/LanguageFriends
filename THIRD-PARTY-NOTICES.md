## Language data

`src/LanguageFriends/wwwroot/data/friends.json` is built by `tools/build_data.py` from the CSV files of the author's Audio project:

| Data | Origin | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Alphabets (`alphabets.csv`) | The letter lists of the EU Publications Office's Formex specification [*Languages, scripts and letters*](https://op.europa.eu/en/web/eu-vocabularies/formex/physical-specifications/character-encoding/languages-scripts-ans-letters), with Ukrainian and Arabic checked against Unicode CLDR `exemplarCharacters` | The list of letters of a language is a fact; EU material is reusable under the [Commission's reuse policy](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2011/833/oj). CLDR data © Unicode, Inc., [Unicode License v3](https://www.unicode.org/license.txt) |
| The numbers 1–10 in words (`numbertext_selected.csv`) | Unicode CLDR rule-based number formatting through the ICU library in Windows, with a few corrections | © Unicode, Inc., [Unicode License v3](https://www.unicode.org/license.txt) |
| The 83 picture words (`dictionary.csv`) | The emoji and Danish words from the author's word list; the translations were written for the Audio project by the author (with AI assistance) and compared with Unicode CLDR emoji keywords | MIT (as part of this project) |
| The game's own texts, greetings and kid names (`Models/Languages.cs`) | Written for this project by the author (with AI assistance) | MIT (as part of this project) |
